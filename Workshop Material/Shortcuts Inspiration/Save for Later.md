# Save for Later

**Share Sheet** · Sorts saved content by URL

When you find something interesting while browsing, you probably save it somewhere - but "somewhere" often means one big unorganized list. This shortcut appears in the Share Sheet and automatically sorts what you save based on the URL.

## What It Does

A Share Sheet shortcut that receives a URL and categorizes it by domain or URL pattern. For example:

- **Videos** → Save to [Play](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1596506190) or a "Watch Later" list
- **Music** → Save to [MusicBox](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1614730313)
- **Recipe sites** → Save to a "Recipes" note or folder
- **Tutorials or news articles** → Add to Safari Reading List or a Read Later app
- **Everything else** → Send to your Mac via [Hyperduck](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1666327168) or prompt you to pick a destination

The categories above are just examples. Think about where *you* save things and what patterns your URLs follow. You might sort by content type, by project, or by urgency. The key insight is that the URL itself often tells you enough to automate the sorting.

## Taking It Further

If you also want to sort files (not just URLs), you could combine this with [Save to Cloud Storage](Save%20to%20Cloud%20Storage.md) into a single "Save" shortcut that first checks whether the input is a URL or a file, then branches accordingly. That way you have one universal save action in your Share Sheet.
