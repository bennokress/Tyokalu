# Ask AI about Input

**Share Sheet** · Send files, websites, or text to an AI for analysis

Sometimes you just want a quick answer about something you're looking at - summarize this article, explain this document, or tell me what's in this image. This shortcut lets you share almost anything to an AI and ask a question about it.

## What It Does

A Share Sheet shortcut that accepts various input types, asks what you want to know, and sends everything to an AI service for analysis:

- **Websites** → Extract the page content and ask the AI about it
- **PDFs & Documents** → Summarize or analyze the text content
- **Images** → Describe what's in the photo or extract text
- **Text** → Translate, rephrase, or explain the selected text

## Taking It Further

Apple Intelligence already offers built-in actions for summarization and text generation. If you need a capability that Apple Intelligence doesn't have - or you simply prefer another provider - you'll need to look into their APIs. Refer to the [Working with APIs](../Tips%20and%20Tricks/Working%20with%20APIs.md) tip for the basics of making HTTP requests in Shortcuts.
