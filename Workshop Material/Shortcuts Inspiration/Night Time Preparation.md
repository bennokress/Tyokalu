# Night Time Preparation

**Complex Setup** · Automate your going-to-sleep and waking-up routines

Going to bed and waking up involve more than just setting an alarm. You might want to adjust your Focus Mode, turn off smart home lights, set a sleep timer, check tomorrow's schedule, or prepare your morning briefing. A pair of shortcuts can handle all of this with a single tap.

## What It Does

Two shortcuts - "Going to Sleep" and "Waking Up" - that bundle everything you do at night and in the morning into one action each. These go far beyond what a simple Sleep Focus Mode schedule can do, because they can interact with calendars, smart home devices, other shortcuts, and more.

## Going to Sleep

This shortcut could handle things like:

- Activating your Sleep Focus Mode (see [Focus Mode Automation](Focus%20Mode%20Automation.md))
- Setting smart home scenes (dim or turn off lights, close blinds, lock doors)
- Setting a wake-up alarm based on tomorrow's first calendar event - maybe even a backup alarm a few minutes later
- Starting a sleep playlist, podcast, or white noise
- Logging the time you went to bed

## Waking Up

This shortcut could handle things like:

- Deactivating Sleep and choosing the right Focus Mode for the day based on your calendar (workday, vacation, weekend, holiday - see [Focus Mode Automation](Focus%20Mode%20Automation.md))
- Turning on lights or opening blinds via smart home
- Showing a summary of today's schedule, weather, and news headlines - or even having Siri read it to you
- Starting your morning playlist or podcast

## Why Shortcuts Instead of Sleep Schedule?

Apple's built-in Sleep Schedule in the Health app can activate Sleep Focus and set alarms, but it follows a fixed schedule. Real life isn't that predictable. A shortcut can check tomorrow's calendar to set the right alarm time, decide which Focus Mode to activate in the morning based on what kind of day it is, and chain together actions across multiple apps and services.
