# Packing List

**Complex Setup** · Generate a personalized packing list for any trip

Packing for a trip always involves the same mental exercise: Where am I going? How long? What's the weather like? Do I need formal clothes? Swim gear? A shortcut can ask these questions once and produce a tailored checklist - no more forgetting your adapter or packing a winter jacket for July.

## What It Does

A shortcut that asks for a trip title, the number of days, and lets you toggle categories on or off. You probably don't need a winter section for your summer vacation - but you still want the bathing suit available for the sauna on your winter trip to the ARCtic Conference in Oulu.

The output is a checklist you can copy into Reminders, Notes, or whatever you use to stay organized.

## Taking It Further

Build category templates as dictionaries: "Winter", "Summer", "Business", "Sports", "Tech" - each with a list of items and quantities that scale with the trip length.
