# Open Password App

**Quick Action** · Jump straight to your password manager

AutoFill handles most password situations, but sometimes you need to open your password manager directly - to copy a one-time code, look up payment details, or deal with apps that don't support AutoFill on some of their input fields. A shortcut gets you there instantly.

## What It Does

A minimal shortcut that opens your password manager app - whether that's Apple's Passwords app (iOS 18+) or a third-party manager like 1Password or Bitwarden. With third-party apps, you could even use their URL schemes to jump straight to a specific section like search.

## Why This Is Worth It

The value here isn't in the shortcut's complexity - it's in the placement. A one-action shortcut in the Control Center or on the Action Button makes switching between your password manager and the target app much smoother. Sometimes the simplest shortcuts save the most time.
