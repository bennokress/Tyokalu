# Workshop Material

This folder contains supplementary material for the Shortcuts workshop. Browse the subfolders for exercises, useful links, and tips to help you on your Shortcuts journey.

## Exercises

This folder contains shortcut and automation ideas organized by skill level (1-5). Each exercise provides inspiration and guidance for building shortcuts that match your current experience level. If you're stuck or not feeling creative, pick an exercise that interests you and follow along.

## Links

Curated resources to help you learn, connect, and enhance your Shortcuts workflow:

- **[Communities](Links/Communities.md)** - Places to ask questions, share your creations, and connect with other Shortcuts enthusiasts
- **[Creators](Links/Creators.md)** - Content creators who regularly share Shortcuts tutorials and inspiration
- **[Tutorials](Links/Tutorials.md)** - Guides and videos to learn the fundamentals and master advanced techniques
- **[Utility Apps](Links/Utility%20Apps.md)** - Apps that extend what's possible with Shortcuts through additional actions and integrations

## Tips and Tricks

Practical tips and tricks for Shortcuts creators. These are the kinds of insights that come from experience - small techniques and best practices that make building and maintaining shortcuts easier and more enjoyable.
