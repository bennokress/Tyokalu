# Battery Low Notification to All Devices

**Automation** · Alert yourself across devices when battery gets low

Your iPhone's battery is dying, but you left it in another room and didn't hear the low-battery chime. This automation sends a push notification to all your devices the moment the battery drops below a threshold.

## What It Does

A battery-level automation that fires when the charge drops below a certain percentage and sends a push notification to your other devices via an external push service like [Pushover](https://pushover.net) (one-time purchase) or [ntfy](https://ntfy.sh) (free and open source). The notification arrives on your iPad, Mac, Apple Watch - wherever you have the push app installed.

## Taking It Further

- Include the device name in the message so you know *which* device is low (useful if you set this up on multiple devices)
- Add a second automation at a lower threshold (e.g., 10%) with higher urgency
