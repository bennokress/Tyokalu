# Connect to Specific Speakers or Headphones

**Quick Action** · Skip the Bluetooth menu and connect directly

Connecting to a specific Bluetooth device normally means opening Settings or Control Center, waiting for the device list, and tapping the right one. A shortcut can do this in a single tap.

## What It Does

A shortcut that connects to a specific audio device directly, skipping the Bluetooth menu entirely. You can make one shortcut per device or a single shortcut with a menu to choose between them. Trigger it from a widget, Siri, or Spotlight.

## Taking It Further

Combine this with other actions to create "mode" shortcuts:

- **Morning routine** → Connect to kitchen speaker + start your favorite playlist
- **Workout** → Connect to AirPods + open your workout app
- **Focus time** → Connect to noise-canceling headphones + enable a Focus Mode
