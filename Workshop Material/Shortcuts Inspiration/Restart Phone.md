# Restart Phone

**Quick Action** · One-tap restart without holding buttons

Restarting your iPhone isn't complicated, but it's surprisingly tedious. Hold the button combination, swipe to turn off, wait for the screen to go black, wait *more* because the phone isn't actually off yet, and then press and hold the power button until the Apple logo appears. We've all been there, anxiously pressing the power button again and again wondering if it's off yet. Siri can do it too ("Restart my phone"), but talking to your phone isn't for everyone or every situation. A shortcut in a widget or the Control Center reduces the whole process to two taps: one to trigger, one to confirm.

## What It Does

A one-action shortcut using the native Restart action. Tap it, confirm, and the phone restarts on its own -- no button holding, no guessing when it's safe to press power again.
