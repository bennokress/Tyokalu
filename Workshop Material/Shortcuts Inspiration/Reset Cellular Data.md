# Reset Cellular Data

**Quick Action** · Quickly cycle your cellular connection

Sometimes your cellular connection gets stuck - slow data, no signal despite full bars, or a failed handoff between towers. Toggling Airplane Mode is the quickest fix, but it involves multiple taps and waiting.

## What It Does

A shortcut that automates the full reset sequence: turn Cellular Data off, enable Airplane Mode, wait about 10 seconds, then turn both back on. One tap and your connection is fresh.
