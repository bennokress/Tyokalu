# The "Show When Run" Toggle

When you add actions from third-party apps to your shortcuts, you'll often notice a toggle called **"Show When Run"**. Understanding this setting can make your shortcuts feel much more polished.

## What It Does

When "Show When Run" is enabled (the default), the Shortcuts app will visually switch to the third-party app while the action executes. You'll see the app open, do its thing, and then return to wherever you were.

When disabled, the action runs in the background. The data is collected or the operation is performed without any visual app-switching - your shortcut continues smoothly.

## When to Turn It Off

Most of the time, you just want the data or result from an action - you don't need to see the app while it works. In these cases, turn "Show When Run" off for a cleaner experience.

For example:
- Fetching data from a third-party app to use later in your shortcut
- Logging something to an app without needing visual confirmation
- Any action where the result matters more than watching it happen

## When to Leave It On

Keep it enabled when you actually need to interact with the app, or when the action's purpose is to open the app at a specific screen or with specific content loaded.

## Finding the Toggle

Tap on any third-party app action in your shortcut to expand its options. If the action supports background execution, you'll see the "Show When Run" toggle among the settings. Not all actions have this option - it depends on how the app developer implemented the action.
