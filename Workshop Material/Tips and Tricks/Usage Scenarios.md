# Usage Scenarios

Shortcuts can be run from the Shortcuts app, of course. But they become far more useful when integrated into different parts of the system.

## Where You Can Run Shortcuts

### Share Sheet

When you tap the Share button in any app, your shortcuts can appear as options. This lets you process content directly - save a link, resize an image, or extract text from a document without leaving the app you're in.

### Home Screen App

Add any shortcut to your Home Screen as an app icon. Tap it, and the shortcut runs. Combine this with a custom icon and name, and it looks and feels like a native app.

### Widgets

Shortcuts can appear in widgets on your:
- **Lock Screen** - Quick access without unlocking
- **Home Screen** - Dedicated shortcuts widget showing multiple options
- **Control Center** - Swipe down and tap to run

### Siri

Trigger shortcuts by voice. Say "Hey Siri" followed by the shortcut's name (or a custom phrase you've assigned). Perfect for hands-free automation.

### Spotlight

Pull down on the Home Screen to open Spotlight, type your shortcut's name, and run it directly from search results.

## Bonus: URL Scheme

Shortcuts can also be triggered from anywhere that can open a URL - push notifications, other apps, QR codes, NFC tags, and more. See [The shortcuts:// URL Scheme](URL%20Scheme.md) for details.
